Metric definitions
------------------
Signed SS Error: mean(q* - q) over the final 100 post-disturbance epochs.
Mean Abs SS Error: mean(abs(q* - q)) over the final 100 post-disturbance epochs.
Settling Time: first post-disturbance epoch at which the absolute error remains inside the configured settling band for 30 consecutive epochs.
Overshoot: maximum queue depth above q* after the workload disturbance.
SS Variance: queue-depth variance over the final 200 post-disturbance epochs.
